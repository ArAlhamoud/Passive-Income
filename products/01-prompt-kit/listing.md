## 2. Description (English)

You know AI tools can save you time, but every time you open ChatGPT or Claude you're not sure what to type, and the result comes out generic: it doesn't sound like your business or your customers.

This kit fixes that. It has 60 prompts written for small businesses and freelancers in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf: Salla and Zid stores, WhatsApp replies, Ramadan, Eid, National Day and Founding Day, Google Maps reviews, and hiring.

Every prompt comes in Modern Standard Arabic and English, with clear [PLACEHOLDERS] for your details. Fill them in, then copy and paste. Prompts for customer-facing replies ask for the output in everyday Saudi dialect.

What you get:
- An A4 PDF that's easy to read on your phone or print.
- 60 prompts in 9 sections. Each one has a title, when to use it, the Arabic prompt, the English prompt and a practical tip.
- A how-to page explaining how to use the prompts and fill in placeholders.
- Clear reminders about what to verify yourself (numbers, Hijri dates, regulations).

An honest note: AI is an assistant and it can make mistakes. This kit helps you get better first drafts faster, but you still need to review them before publishing. It is not legal or accounting advice.
